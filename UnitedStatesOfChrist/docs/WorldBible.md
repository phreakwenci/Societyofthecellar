# World Bible — United States of Christ

Living reference for the fictional theocracy. Grow this as extraction
proceeds; every claim about the world should ultimately trace to a law
record in `data/json/`.

## Premise

At some point in the near future, a constitutional convention — called for
narrower reasons and captured wholesale — ratified the **Covenant
Amendment**: "The Holy Bible, being the revealed will of Almighty God, is
the supreme law of the United States of Christ, any thing in the
Constitution or laws of any State notwithstanding." Everything else in
this project is the paperwork that sentence generates.

## The central engine

The Bible is not a legal code; it is several legal codes, from different
centuries, embedded in narrative, poetry, and letters, that disagree with
each other. A state that claims to enforce all of it must actually choose
— and every choice is a confession. The USofC's fingerprints:

- **Enforced loudly:** sexual purity codes, Sabbath, tithing, blasphemy,
  obedience to authority (Romans 13 is functionally the state motto).
- **Deferred indefinitely:** Jubilee debt cancellation (Lev 25), interest
  prohibitions (Ex 22:25), gleaning rights (Lev 19:9), welcoming the
  stranger (Lev 19:34), sell-all-you-have (Mt 19:21).
- **Harmonized away:** the Sermon on the Mount, which the Committee on
  Scriptural Consistency has classified as "eschatological counsel, not
  civil statute."

The gap between the first list and the second is the plot of every story
set in this world.

## Structural notes

- **Federalism survived, mutated.** States are now "Tribes" for certain
  ceremonial purposes; the tribal framing lets the regime map Israelite
  law onto American geography.
- **The two-witness rule** (Deut 19:15) governs all prosecution. In
  practice it created a professional witness industry and a market price
  for corroboration — the single most corrupting institution in the
  country.
- **Cities of Refuge** (Num 35): six designated federal cities where
  accused killers can outrun avengers-of-blood. De facto: America's
  strangest municipalities, part sanctuary, part open-air prison.
- **The calendar is law.** Sabbaths, new moons, and three pilgrimage
  festivals (Ex 23:14-17) structure the fiscal and civic year. The
  logistics of Passover travel mandates are a running national crisis.

## Tone

Bureaucratic realism over dystopian grand guignol. The horror is in the
forms, the licensing queues, the inspector's clipboard, the neighbor who
reports you — and in how reasonable everyone sounds. Comedy and dread
share every scene.
