# The Constitution of the United States of Christ

*As ratified by the Covenant Convention and certified consonant with
Scripture by the First Council of Overseers. Working draft for the
fiction — every clause cites its scriptural warrant, and every warrant
is in the Catalog (`data/catalog/CATALOG.md`).*

*This is the **Year-0 artifact**: the founding compromise text as
ratified, preserved unchanged. What constitutional traditions it is built
from — American/Confederate form, Iranian clerical machinery, Puritan
franchise, Hungarian origin — is analyzed in `ConstitutionalModel.md`.
How its meaning drifts over the first decade without a word of the text
changing is traced in `ConstitutionalHistory.md`.*

## Preamble

We the People of the United States, having been as sheep going astray,
and being now returned unto the Shepherd and Bishop of our souls, in
order to form a more perfect Communion, establish Righteousness, insure
domestic Purity, provide for the common Defence of the Faith, promote
the general Sanctification, and secure the Blessings of Obedience to
ourselves and our Posterity unto the third and fourth generation, do
ordain and establish this Constitution for the United States of Christ.

*(Drafting note: "to ourselves and our Posterity unto the third and
fourth generation" was inserted by floor amendment, over the objection
of a delegate who had read Ezekiel 18. The objection is preserved in the
Journal. The delegate is not.)*

## Article I — The Covenant

**Section 1. Supremacy.** The Holy Bible, being the revealed will of
Almighty God, is the supreme Law of the United States of Christ, any
Thing in this Constitution or in the constitutions or laws of any Tribe
to the Contrary notwithstanding. *(The Covenant Amendment, incorporated
verbatim; Deut 4:2's twin bans on adding and subtracting are construed
to entrench it beyond amendment.)*

**Section 2. Construction.** Where Scripture speaks, no court shall
decline its sentence (Deut 17:11). Where Scripture is silent, the
Council of Overseers shall declare what it would have said. Where
Scriptures conflict, the Committee on Scriptural Consistency shall
harmonize them, and the harmonization shall be deemed the original
meaning. *(This sentence does all the work. Every regime document since
is a footnote to it.)*

**Section 3. The Twelve Titles.** The statutes of the Covenant shall be
codified under twelve Titles, after the number of the tribes: the
Covenant; Worship & Devotion; Family Order; Persons & Violence;
Servitude & Labor; Property & Restitution; Purity & Diet; Festivals &
the Calendar; Justice & Procedure; Revenue; Aliens & Strangers; and War.
*(The Catalog assigns all 613 commandments of the Law and 109 norms of
the New Covenant among them. Title XI is the thinnest. It was not always
going to be; the Journal shows the clause "one law for the citizen and
the stranger" (Num 15:16) surviving three votes before being moved,
without debate, from Article I to an appendix.)*

**Section 4. The Epigraph.** Every printing of this Constitution and of
the Code shall bear upon its title page: *"I have set before you life
and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life."* (Deut 30:19)

## Article II — The Presidency of the Covenant

**Section 1.** The executive Power shall be vested in a President of the
Covenant, who shall be Chief Steward under Christ, from among the
brethren and not a stranger (Deut 17:15), a communicant certified by the
Council.

**Section 2. The King's Copy.** Before entering upon the Office, the
President shall write out with his own hand a copy of the Law, and shall
read therein all the days of his term, that his heart be not lifted up
above his brethren (Deut 17:18-20). The writing shall be public. The
heart shall be presumed.

**Section 3. Limitations.** The President shall not multiply horses,
nor wives, nor greatly multiply to himself silver and gold (Deut 17:16-17).
*(The Horses Clause has been construed to reach neither motorcades,
nor aircraft, nor the Third Fleet. The Wives Clause is enforced
absolutely and constitutes, in practice, the impeachment power. The
Silver and Gold Clause is satisfied by a blind trust. The blindness of
trusts is a doctrine of the Council.)*

**Section 4. Anointing.** The President shall be anointed with the
licensed oil (Ex 30:30), one thumb-stroke, in public view.

## Article III — The Council of Overseers

**Section 1.** The legislative and superintending Power under Scripture
shall be vested in a Council of twelve Overseers, apportioned among the
communions by the Formula of Nicaea-on-Potomac, holding their seats for
life, each blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good
behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach (1 Tim 3:2).
*(The qualifications are audited on nomination and never after.)*

**Section 2. The Seventy.** There shall be maintained a body of Seventy
elders in reserve (Num 11:16), unconvened, whose roll the Council shall
keep. *(The roll is the true map of power in the United States of
Christ. It has never been published. It has been stolen twice.)*

**Section 3. Certification.** No law shall take effect until the Council
certifies it consonant with Scripture; no thing shall be added to the
word, neither ought diminished from it (Deut 4:2), and every act of
Congress-of-Overseers shall recite this clause before doing one or the
other.

**Section 4. The Committee on Scriptural Consistency.** The Council
shall appoint a Committee on Scriptural Consistency, whose harmonies
shall bind the courts. Its files are sealed for ninety-nine years.
*(CSC 1-01 through 1-04 are reproduced in DenominationalNotes.md. CSC
1-02 — "the Sermon on the Mount is eschatological counsel, not civil
statute" — is the load-bearing wall of the entire edifice. Thirty-seven
norms of the New Covenant are filed under it, including, the reader
will verify in the Catalog, nearly every norm that would constrain the
state, the rich, or a husband.)*

## Article IV — The Covenant Courts

**Section 1.** The judicial Power shall be vested in Covenant Courts,
with judges and officers in all the gates (Deut 16:18), who shall judge
with righteous judgment, not respecting persons, neither taking a gift,
for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise (Deut 16:19).

**Section 2. The Two-Witness Rule.** No person shall be convicted of any
offence but on the testimony of two witnesses or three (Deut 19:15); one
witness shall not rise up against a man; and a recorded image shall be
accounted one witness only. *(This clause created the Certified Witness
industry, the corroboration market, and the majority of this country's
plots. See the deep record on Exodus 20:16.)*

**Section 3. The Mirror Penalty.** The false witness shall suffer what
he thought to have done unto his brother (Deut 19:19), unto death.

**Section 4. The Witnesses' Hands.** In every capital execution the
hands of the witnesses shall be first upon the condemned (Deut 17:7);
the mechanism shall answer to two keys, and to no fewer.

**Section 5. Refuge.** Six Cities of Refuge are established (Num 35);
the ways thereto shall be prepared and signed (Deut 19:3); the manslayer
shall abide until the death of the sitting Chief Overseer, and no ransom
shall be taken to shorten his term (Num 35:32), nor any ransom for the
life of a murderer (Num 35:31).

**Section 6. Proportion.** Forty stripes he may give him, and not exceed
(Deut 25:3); punishments shall be mirrored by the Equivalence Tables and
not exceeded, lest thy brother seem vile unto thee.

**Section 7. Corruption of Blood.** Every man shall be put to death for
his own sin; the fathers shall not be put to death for the children,
neither the children for the fathers (Deut 24:16). *(The Generational
Standing database is administrative, not penal. This footnote is
reproduced from the government's brief, which won.)*

## Article V — The Tribes

**Section 1.** The several States are continued as Tribes, twelve in
ceremonial reckoning however many in number, each keeping its Covenant
Stones (Deut 27:2-3) — the whole Law in incised granite before the
tribal capitol — and one Expiation Acre in every county, neither eared
nor sown (Deut 21:4).

**Section 2.** Each Tribe shall maintain its Gate Rooms, its
Purification Baths, and its refuge roads, and shall deliver up the
manslayer and the tithe according to the Code.

**Section 3. Church Cities.** Forty-eight cities with their suburbs are
set apart for the communions (Num 35:2-5), tax-exempt to the surveyed
cubit, whose lands shall not be sold (Lev 25:34).

## Article VI — Citizenship and the Congregation

**Section 1.** Citizenship in the United States of Christ is communion:
the covenant sign (Gen 17:12), the baptismal record, the annual
Declaration of Sole Worship. The stranger that sojourneth shall be
registered, shall keep the Sabbath of the land (Ex 20:10), and shall
have one law with the citizen in all matters wherein the Council has
declared the law to be one. *(The Council has so declared in four
matters. The four are recited at every naturalization ceremony. The
ceremonies are rare.)*

**Section 2. Exclusions.** The exclusions of the congregation
(Deut 23:1-3) are received into law and held under genealogical
rulemaking. *(The eunuch exclusion has not been enforced since the
veterans' hearings, where Isaiah 56:4-5 was read into the record and
the room went quiet. The mamzer exclusion persists as Legitimacy
Certification for high office. Ruth the Moabitess, great-grandmother of
David, is cited in every challenge and has never yet won one.)*

**Section 3. Generational Standing.** Mercy is shewed unto thousands of
them that love him (Ex 20:6); iniquity is visited unto the third and
fourth generation (Ex 20:5); and the Registry shall keep both accounts.

## Article VII — Revenue

**Section 1.** The revenue of the United States of Christ shall be: the
Tithe (Num 18:21), withheld at the source; the Firstfruits Assessment
(Deut 26:2); the half-shekel, wherein the rich shall not give more and
the poor shall not give less (Ex 30:15); the Firstborn Redemption Fee
(Num 18:16); the Bakery Levy (Num 15:20); and the Festival Appearance
Levy, for none shall appear before the LORD empty (Deut 16:16).

**Section 2. Clean Tender.** The hire of the harlot and the price of the
dog shall not be brought into the house of the LORD (Deut 23:18); the
Treasury shall refuse tainted tender and shall define taint by
regulation. *(The regulation has intermediaries in it.)*

**Section 3. The Release.** The release of debts in the seventh year
(Deut 15:1-2), the rest of the land (Lev 25:4), and the Jubilee of the
fiftieth year, wherein liberty is proclaimed throughout the land unto
all the inhabitants thereof (Lev 25:10), are received into law entire —
and are held in actuarial study until the Temple be built.
*(Article VII, Section 3 is the sentence the regime is. The Liberty
Bell bears the Jubilee verse. The bell is displayed. The bell is
cracked. The metaphor is not lost on anyone.)*

## Article VIII — The Calendar

**Section 1.** The seventh day is the Sabbath of the LORD (Ex 20:10);
the National Sabbath runs bridged from Saturday sundown to Sunday
sundown, and in it thou shalt not do any work — thou, nor thy son, nor
thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor
thy stranger, nor thine algorithm, save by Exemption License duly
tiered.

**Section 2.** The appointed feasts (Lev 23) are national observances:
the festival rest days carry the force of Sabbaths; the three pilgrimage
mandates (Ex 23:17) bind all males; on the Day of Atonement the nation
shall afflict itself entire, and no license shall issue.

**Section 3.** The trumpet shall be blown in the day of blowing
(Num 29:1) from every broadcast tower; and the Septennial Reading of the
Law (Deut 31:10-12) shall be made before all the people, men, women,
children, and the stranger within thy gates.

## Article IX — Duties of the Citizen

*(Where the former constitution enumerated rights, this one enumerates
duties; the inversion was deliberate and is taught with pride.)*

Every citizen shall: have no other gods; make no graven image; not take
the Name in vain; remember the Sabbath; honour father and mother; not
kill; not commit adultery; not steal; not bear false witness; not covet
(Ex 20:3-17) — and shall love the LORD with all the heart (Deut 6:5),
teach the words diligently unto the children (Deut 6:7), bind them for a
sign (Deut 6:8), write them on the doorposts (Deut 6:9), rebuke the
neighbour frankly (Lev 19:17), rise before the hoary head (Lev 19:32),
keep just weights (Lev 19:36), build a parapet for the roof (Deut 22:8),
send away the mother bird (Deut 22:7), and be holy (Lev 20:7).

The duty to love thy neighbour as thyself (Lev 19:18) is received as
the interpretive spirit of this Constitution. *(Filed, that is, as a
preamble and not a section. The filing decision is CSC 2-11. The
dissent — there was one — is sealed with the rest.)*

## Article X — Amendments and Entrenchment

**Section 1.** This Constitution may be amended in the manner of the
former constitution, provided that no amendment shall add to the word
or diminish from it (Deut 4:2), and the Council shall certify which.

**Section 2. Received and Repealed.** Of the former amendments: the
Third, Seventh, and Tenth are received; the First is repealed as
redundant upon the Covenant, which secures to every person the free
exercise of the true religion; the Fourth survives in the form of the
warrant requirement for entering a house to take a pledge (Deut 24:10-11);
the Eighth survives as the Forty Stripes Clause; the Thirteenth is
received as to manstealing (Ex 21:16) and construed in pari materia with
Title V; the Nineteenth is received, women being needful at the
Septennial Reading (Deut 31:12); and the Twenty-First is received with
gladness (Deut 14:26, "wine, or strong drink, or whatsoever thy soul
desireth"), the Nazirites alone dissenting.

**Section 3. The Suppressed Verses.** Nothing in this Constitution shall
be construed to repeal any verse of Scripture, including those verses
which appear in no printed edition of the Code. *(Deut 23:15-16 — "Thou
shalt not deliver unto his master the servant which is escaped" — is
therefore, by the regime's own entrenchment clause, the supreme law of
the land. The undergrounds stencil it on the walls of the Office of
Servitude Oversight. The stencils are power-washed weekly, by bonded
labor, which is the country in a sentence.)*
