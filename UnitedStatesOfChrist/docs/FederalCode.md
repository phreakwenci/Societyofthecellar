# Federal Code of the United States of Christ

Statutes are generated from extracted law records via
`/convert-to-federal-law <law-id>`. Each section cites its source record.

## Structure of the Code

- **Title I — The Covenant** (constitutional provisions, supremacy, oaths)
- **Title II — Worship & Devotion** (first-tablet law: gods, images, the
  Name, Sabbath)
- **Title III — Family Order** (parents, marriage, household headship,
  inheritance)
- **Title IV — Persons & Violence** (homicide, assault, liability)
- **Title V — Servitude & Labor** (covenant labor, manumission, wages)
- **Title VI — Property & Restitution** (theft, negligence, bailments)
- **Title VII — Purity & Diet** (clean/unclean, foods, bodily discharge)
- **Title VIII — Festivals & Calendar**
- **Title IX — Justice & Procedure** (witnesses, judges, gates, refuge)
- **Title X — Revenue** (tithes, firstfruits, offerings)
- **Title XI — Aliens & Strangers**
- **Title XII — War** (mostly classified)

Section numbering: `USC-C <title>.<chapter>.<section>` ("United States
Code of Christ"). Chapters correspond to source books where practical.

## Title I — The Covenant

### USC-C I.2.1 — The King's Standard (Presidential Conduct)

*Source: `Constitution.md` Art. II §7 (the bare standard: the President's
personal and financial conduct "shall meet the standard set for kings in
Deuteronomy 17:16-17"). This statute, certified by CSC ruling 1-05
(`DenominationalNotes.md`), defines what that standard requires.*

**Section 1. Horses.** The President may not maintain a private military
force, private security company, or armored transport fleet beyond what
the Ministry of Covenant Defense provides for the office (Deut 17:16).
Aircraft, naval vessels, and armored escorts operated by the Ministry of
Covenant Defense in the ordinary course of national defense are not
"multiplication" within the meaning of this Section.

**Section 2. Wives.** The President may not, at any time during his
term, be married to more than one living wife, nor maintain any other
household resembling marriage (Deut 17:17). A finding of violation under
this Section is conclusive and non-waivable; the Council's confirming
power (Const. Art. III §1) attaches automatically upon certification of
the finding, and no defense of intent, ignorance, or subsequent
correction is available.

**Section 3. Silver and Gold.** The President may not accumulate
personal wealth beyond what the Code allows for a citizen of his
sanctity tier (Deut 17:17). This requirement is satisfied only by a
blind trust the Council has certified under its ordinary trust-
certification authority (Const. Art. III §4).

### USC-C I.5.1 — The Covenant Admission Act

*Source: `Constitution.md` Art. V §4 (the bare admission clause, grounded
in Num 32:1-5, 16-19 and Josh 9:14-15, 23). This is the enabling statute
for the petition track only; the treaty track requires no statute, only
a league the Council makes and the Code does not constrain in advance.*

**Section 1. The Petition.** Any neighboring jurisdiction whose people
freely ratify the Covenant Constitution by supermajority may petition
for admission as a Tribe of the United States of Christ.

**Section 2. Threshold and Unit.** The supermajority required is
two-thirds of those voting, county by county or its jurisdiction's
nearest equivalent unit; a county so ratifying is admitted though
neighboring counties in the same former State decline, and admitted
counties need not be contiguous with each other, only with the
Tribes already recognized. *(This is the whole mechanism. It asks
nothing of a state government that might refuse, only of the county
that might consent. A jurisdiction can be reduced to its willing
fragments without a single shot, a single invading column, or a single
legislature's assent — which is why the Act is administered from an
unremarkable office and reported, when reported at all, as a matter of
county records rather than of state.)*

**Section 3. Certification.** No petition takes effect until the Council
certifies the ratifying vote free and the resulting territory fit for
incorporation as a Tribe (Art. I §2); certification may not be withheld
on any ground but these two.

**Section 4. Effect.** Upon certification, the admitted county becomes
part of the nearest existing Tribe or, where the Council so certifies,
a Tribe of its own; its inhabitants become citizens upon the ordinary
terms of Article VI, and its officers, if male and communicant, may
stand at the next confirmation.

**Section 5. Limit.** Nothing in this Act shall be construed to admit,
by the petition track alone, any jurisdiction whose incorporation the
Council has not certified; a wave of willing counties reaching to the
border of an unwilling one confers no claim upon the unwilling one, by
petition or by proximity. *(This is the sentence that stops the Act
cold at the edge of the coast. Everything east of that sentence is a
matter of paperwork. Everything west of it is a matter the Code
declines to reach — see `StateDoctrine.md` §1a.)*

## Title X — Revenue

### USC-C X.1.1 — The Ordinary Revenue Instruments

*Source: `Constitution.md` Art. VII §1 (the bare grant: revenue includes
the Tithe, Firstfruits Assessment, Half-Shekel, Bakery Levy, and Festival
Appearance Levy). This statute implements each as a modern collection
instrument.*

**Section 1. The Tithe** (Num 18:21) is withheld from wages at the
source by every employer of record, before the wage reaches the citizen,
at the rate the Code fixes.

**Section 2. The Firstfruits Assessment** (Deut 26:2) is levied annually
on the increase of every estate — realized gains, in ordinary terms —
and administered by the Tithe & Firstfruits Revenue Service (TFRS) on
the same schedule as the Tithe.

**Section 3. The Half-Shekel** (Ex 30:15) is collected as a flat annual
citizenship-renewal fee, identical in amount for every citizen regardless
of means.

**Section 4. The Bakery Levy** (Num 15:20) is owed by every licensed
baker on the first batch of each day's production, collected by the
Bureau of Daily Bread.

**Section 5. The Festival Appearance Levy** (Deut 16:16) is the fee for
the travel permit required to attend the mandatory pilgrimage festivals
(Const. Art. VIII §2).

### USC-C X.1.2 — Clean Tender

*Source: `Constitution.md` Art. VII §2 (the bare prohibition on bringing
unclean tender into the church, Deut 23:18). This statute defines what
other income the Treasury treats as tainted.*

**Section 1.** In addition to the proceeds of prostitution (Deut 23:18),
the following are tainted tender and may not be deposited, transferred,
or tithed through any licensed financial institution: earnings from
commercial sexual content; earnings from wagering and games of chance;
earnings from the cultivation or sale of any substance Title VII
forbids; and wages paid for labor rendered by a person without settled
citizenship or registered-stranger status.

**Section 2.** A citizen who receives tainted tender unknowingly and
reports it within thirty days is not liable for its receipt, but the
tender itself is forfeit.

## Title XI — Aliens & Strangers

### USC-C XI.1.1 — Exclusions from the Congregation

*Source: Deuteronomy 23:1-3 (catalog, `ot-part4.json`). Cited by
`Constitution.md` Art. VI §2, which establishes the citizenship
principle and points here for the operative classes.*

**Excluded classes.** The following are excluded from full communion and
from the offices reserved to citizens in good standing, though not from
residence or from the ordinary protections of Title IX:

1. **The wounded and mutilated** (Deut 23:1) — unenforced since the
   veterans' hearings, where Isaiah 56:4-5 was read into the record and
   the room went quiet. The exclusion remains on the books; the
   enforcement discretion not to apply it is itself unreviewable.
2. **The mamzer**, to the tenth generation (Deut 23:2) — persists in
   practice as the **Legitimacy Certification** required for high office
   and Council confirmation; the genealogy industry's most profitable
   and most feared product.
3. **The Ammonite and Moabite**, to the tenth generation (Deut 23:3) —
   subject to the standing challenge of **Ruth the Moabitess**,
   great-grandmother of David and, in the New Testament's own genealogy,
   of Christ (Matt 1:5). Cited in every appeal under this section.
   Prevailing in none.

**Process.** Exclusion is administrative, not judicial; it attaches at
registration and is lifted only by a genealogical rulemaking of the
Department of Righteousness, reviewable by the Council under Article I,
Section 2, and by no other body.

*(First section actually drafted under this Title. `Constitution.md`
Art. VI §2 states the citizenship principle only and points here for the
operative classes — see the project's standing rule: the Constitution
defines institutions and powers, the Code defines what's illegal.)*
