# The Model of the Constitution

*A worldbuilding reference: what real constitutional traditions the
Constitution of the United States of Christ (`Constitution.md`) is built
from, why, and how the pieces fit. Written in the register of an in-world
comparative-law essay — the kind an émigré scholar publishes abroad and a
domestic one publishes at his peril.*

---

## The thesis: a palimpsest, not a blueprint

A country that secedes from the United States does not invent a
constitution from nothing. It writes the one it already has in its hands,
and edits. So the Constitution of the United States of Christ is a
**palimpsest** — American parchment, scraped and overwritten, with the old
script still legible underneath. Its genius, and its instability, is that
it borrows a different thing from each of four traditions and pretends
they are one.

Read the layers from the outside in:

### 1. Form → the United States, by way of the Confederacy (1787 / 1861)

The *shape* is American. Written, single-document, entrenched, supreme over
the states, enforced by judicial review, embedded in a common-law culture
of precedent. The direct template is not the 1787 Constitution but the one
previous American secession that actually rewrote it: the **Confederate
Constitution of 1861**, which copied the federal text almost verbatim,
edited the ideologically load-bearing clauses, and — the tell — added God
to the Preamble the framers had left secular ("invoking the favor and
guidance of Almighty God").

The USofC does exactly this. Its Preamble is the 1787 cadence rewritten in
Scripture ("We the People of the United States, having been as sheep going
astray…"). Its **Article X §2** does not abolish the Bill of Rights; it
*adjudicates* it clause by clause — receiving the Third, Seventh, and
Tenth, repealing the First "as redundant upon the Covenant," keeping the
Fourth as a pledge-seizure warrant rule. That is the Confederate method:
inherit the structure, launder the content. Federalism survives as the
**Tribes** (Art. V). The amendment machinery survives (Art. X §1). Even the
habit of a written supremacy clause survives — pointed now at Scripture
instead of at the states.

*Why it matters dramatically:* the retained American forms are the
regime's alibi. "We kept the Constitution" is true on its face and false in
every operation, and the gap is deniable precisely because the parchment is
familiar.

### 2. Power structure → Iran (1979)

The *machinery* is Iranian, and this is the layer the regime least likes
named. The Islamic Republic solved the problem of putting clerics above a
republic without abolishing the republic, and the USofC copied the
solution part for part:

| Iran (1979) | United States of Christ |
|---|---|
| Supreme Leader (*velayat-e faqih*) | **Chief Overseer**, presiding over the Council |
| Guardian Council — vets all legislation *and* all candidates against Islam | **Council of Overseers (COO)** — certifies all law "consonant with Scripture," vets candidates (Art. III) |
| Expediency Discernment Council — resolves deadlock, "harmonizes" | **Committee on Scriptural Consistency (CSC)** — harmonizes contradictions, and its harmonies bind the courts (Art. III §5) |
| Assembly of Experts — clerics who elect and (in theory) remove the Leader | **The Seventy** (Art. III §3) — the unconvened reserve body whose roll "is the true map of power" |
| Elected President, subordinate | **Presidency of the Covenant (POTC)** — "certified by the Council" (Art. II §1) |

The elected branch exists and is real; it is simply not supreme. Above it
sits an unelected clerical apparatus that can void any law and any
candidate by declaring them inconsistent with the sacred text — and a
second body whose whole function is to make the inconsistencies go away.
**CSC 1-02** (the Sermon on the Mount is "eschatological counsel, not civil
statute") is the *velayat-e faqih* move in miniature: the cleric's reading
overrides the plain text, and there is no appeal above the cleric.

*Why it matters dramatically:* everything the citizen votes for can be
un-decided by men the citizen did not elect, on grounds the citizen may
not read (the CSC files are sealed ninety-nine years). The ballot is
sincere and the power is elsewhere.

### 3. Self-image → Puritan New England (Massachusetts Bay, 1630)

The *mythology* is neither American-secular nor Iranian-clerical. It is
Puritan, and it is the layer the regime talks about constantly. A Christian
America does not imagine itself as Tehran; it imagines itself as Winthrop's
**city upon a hill** — a covenanted people, an errand into the wilderness,
a nation in a treaty with God whose terms are the blessings and the curses.

The concrete inheritance is the **franchise**. In Massachusetts Bay the
vote belonged to church members ("freemen"); citizenship *was* communion.
The USofC's **Article VI** is that clause resurrected: citizenship is the
covenant sign, the baptismal record, the annual Declaration of Sole
Worship. The deep Protestant ancestors stand behind it — **Calvin's Geneva**
(the Consistory, moral surveillance, the godly city-state, 1541) and
**Cromwell's Instrument of Government** (1653), England's only written
constitution, drafted under a Puritan military-religious Protectorate. The
USofC's moral-enforcement bureaus (the SSC, the OIP, the NDS confession
assemblies) are the Genevan Consistory with better databases.

*Why it matters dramatically:* the regime's sincerity lives here. The
people who built it are not cynics playing Iran; they are believers playing
Winthrop, which is far more dangerous, because a cynic can be bargained
with and a covenanter cannot.

### 4. Origin mechanism → Brexit (2016), hardening into Hungary (2011)

The *how* — how a nation leaves one order and enters another without a
revolution — is **Brexit**, not a coup. Secession from the United States
came the way Britain left the European Union: **by referendum.** A single
national vote, a propaganda campaign, a narrow margin, and then years of
messy extraction whose real cost only became visible a decade on.

**But a referendum needed a door to walk through first, and 1869's *Texas
v. White* had nailed it shut.** `ConstitutionalHistory.md`'s "Before Year
0" section supplies the legal precursor Brexit itself never needed
(Britain always had an exit clause; America's Union was declared
"indissoluble"): a coalition of rural states reads the real 2024
overturning of the *Chevron* doctrine as reopening judicial review of the
"consent of the States" language *Texas v. White* left dangling, tests it
in a fictional consolidated case (*Idaho v. United States*, "the
Renegotiation Ruling"), and wins nothing but standing — the right to
*ask.* The Referendum is the asking. Brexit supplies the form the
campaign takes; the Renegotiation Ruling supplies the reason anyone
believed the campaign could possibly work.

The Brexit playbook, feature for feature:

- A **referendum**, not a convention first. The people were *asked* — "shall
  the nation return to God and leave the secular union?" — and answered.
  Everything the regime does afterward rests on that Yes. The Covenant
  Referendum is the regime's unanswerable card: *you voted for this.*
- A **sovereignty slogan** doing enormous work. "Take Back Control" becomes
  **"Take Back the Nation for Christ"**; a bewildering constitutional
  rupture is sold as a simple act of restoration. The bus, the border
  poster, the promise that the tithe would fund every hospital — each has a
  scriptural analogue, and each outran its own fine print.
- A **margin, not a mandate.** It won ~52–48, and — unlike Brexit, which
  split by *place* — this split by *belief*, so the fault line ran through
  congregations and kitchens, not regions. Half the country is governed by
  a covenant it voted against.
- A **long, disillusioning extraction.** Disentangling from federal law,
  currency, courts, and trade took years; the shortages, the exit of the
  skilled and the doubtful, the slow admission that it cost more than the
  bus promised — all of it maps onto Brexit's real afterlife, and all of it
  is denied by a political class for whom reversal is now heresy.

**Then Hungary.** Once *outside*, the internal capture followed Orbán's 2011
**Fundamental Law**: a Christian-nationalist preamble, courts packed, and
**cardinal laws** — statutes entrenched behind a supermajority so that one
government's policy becomes the next government's constitution. In the USofC
these become **Cornerstone Certifications** (`ConstitutionalHistory.md`).
Brexit got the nation *out*; Hungary is how the people who ran the Leave
campaign made the new order impossible to leave in turn.

*Why it matters dramatically:* the horror begins with a *ballot*. There is
no junta to blame, no day the tanks rolled — only a majority, on a Tuesday,
and the neighbors who voted the other way and were governed anyway. Every
character either voted for this or lives among those who did.

## The four schools: how religious scholars wanted to structure it

No constitution was ever going to satisfy the coalition that wrote this
one, because "religious constitutional scholars" is not one party. It is at
least four, and they map onto the denominational blocs already fighting
over the Council (`DenominationalNotes.md`). Each wanted a different
country. The document is the treaty between them, which is why it satisfies
none and contradicts itself on schedule.

### School I — The Theonomists ("the Bible needs no ratification")

*Reformed bloc.* The Christian-Reconstruction position (its real-world
father is R. J. Rushdoony's *Institutes of Biblical Law*): the civil law of
Moses, penal sanctions and all, binds every nation, and a *written human
constitution above Scripture is itself idolatry* — a graven text set
beside the Word. Their ideal is minimal: a one-line supremacy clause and
then the Law itself, unmediated. They control the CSC, so they lost the
argument about whether a constitution should exist and won the argument
about who gets to interpret it — the most Reformed outcome imaginable.

Their unresolved bomb is **Article I §2 + Deuteronomy 4:2**: if nothing may
be added to the Word, on what authority does the *Constitution* exist? They
signed the document while denying it should. That signature is the fault
line the whole history runs along.

### School II — The Evangelical Populists ("a godly man, chosen by the people")

*Evangelical bloc.* Suspicious of clerical councils as crypto-Catholic,
they wanted the Hungarian answer: a strong elected President who *embodies*
the nation's faith, ratified and re-ratified by acclamation, revival as a
mode of government. Plebiscite over magisterium; the anointed leader over
the standing committee. They got the Presidency (Art. II) and the media and
the NDS — and then watched the Council they distrusted acquire the power to
certify their anointed man out of eligibility. Their grievance is the
engine of the Supremacy Crisis in `ConstitutionalHistory.md`.

### School III — The Magisterialists ("a settled teaching authority")

*Liturgical / high-church bloc — Catholic, Orthodox, high Anglican
survivors.* They wanted Rome or Tehran in the structural sense: a genuine
magisterium, conciliar and continuous, reasoning from natural law rather
than proof-texting, with subsidiarity below and canon law throughout.
They are the reason the Council of Overseers exists as a *standing
conciliar body* at all rather than a populist plebiscite — the one
structural fight they won, and the reason they're tolerated, surveilled,
and indispensable (they staff the hospitals; see `DenominationalNotes.md`).

### School IV — The Old Republicans ("ordered liberty is also biblical")

*The constitutionalist remnant.* Not a denomination but a temperament:
lawyers and officeholders who argued that due process, the amendment
process, and judicial review are themselves biblical goods (Romans 13's
ordered authority; the framers as latter-day Deuteronomists), and who
fought to preserve as much of the 1787 structure as a brake on the other
three. They wrote **Article X §2** — the clause-by-clause reception of the
old amendments — and **Article IV** — the two-witness rule as a real
evidentiary bar. Every retained American protection is their fingerprint.
They are losing, one certification at a time, and the record of their
losing is the history that follows.

**Who won the founding, and who is winning now.** The ratified document is
a **Magisterialist–Old Republican compromise**: a standing clerical council
(School III) checked by retained American forms (School IV), with the
Evangelical Presidency (School II) as the popular front and the Theonomists
(School I) staffing the interpretive machinery they distrusted on
principle. But the compromise is unstable in one specific direction — the
Theonomists hold the CSC, and the CSC's harmonies bind the courts. The
faction that thinks the constitution shouldn't exist controls the office
that says what it means. Over the decade, that is the hand on the tiller.
Which is the subject of `ConstitutionalHistory.md`.

## For the writer

The palimpsest is a plot generator. Any scene can be located by asking
*which layer is showing through right now*:

- An American-forms scene: a defense lawyer wins on the Fourth Amendment's
  pledge-seizure survival (Art. X §2) — the old script, briefly legible.
- An Iranian-machinery scene: a certified election result is voided by the
  Council on a sealed CSC finding — the middle layer asserting itself.
- A Puritan-mythology scene: a Covenant Renewal assembly, sincere and vast,
  Winthrop's sermon read aloud — the regime meaning every word.
- A Brexit-origin scene: referendum night, the Leave party, the slogan on
  the wall — a family cheering the vote that will take each of them in turn.
- A Hungarian-capture scene: a committee markup where a Cornerstone
  Certification is entrenched by supermajority — the country being made
  impossible to leave, on the record.

The best scenes are where two layers contradict in the same room: the
sincere covenanter (Layer 3) operating the clerical veto (Layer 2) through
the retained American form (Layer 1) that the Theonomist beside him
(School I) believes is a graven idol. Everyone has a citation. Nobody is
lying. That is the country.
