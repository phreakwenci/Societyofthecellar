# State Doctrine of the United States of Christ

*The governance model beneath the Constitution and the Catalog: how the
USOC is actually run day to day, who has power, what is federal, and what
an ordinary family lives under. Written to serve the play — every section
ends by naming the family beats it drives. Companion to
`Constitution.md` (the law), `ConstitutionalModel.md` (the sources),
`ConstitutionalHistory.md` (the drift), and `Agencies.md` (the org chart).*

---

## 0. How it began — the Covenant Referendum (Brexit, not coup)

The USOC did not seize power; **the people voted for it.** A national
referendum — the Covenant Referendum — asked whether the nation should
"return to God" and leave the secular federal union. The Leave campaign
ran on **"Take Back the Nation for Christ,"** the sacred cousin of "Take
Back Control": a complex constitutional rupture sold as a simple act of
restoration. There was a bus (the tithe "will fund every church and
hospital"). There was a border poster (moral decline at the gates). There
was a Project Fear the faithful were taught to dismiss.

It won narrowly — and, unlike Brexit, it split along **belief, not
geography**, so the 52/48 line ran *through congregations and kitchens*.
Then came years of messy extraction and the slow discovery of what had
actually been voted for. The play opens after the vote, in Year 1, as the
extraction becomes law. (Full arc: `ConstitutionalHistory.md`.)

*Drives:* the family voted Leave. Everything that happens to them, they
chose. That is the play's original wound.

## 1. What is federal, and what is left to the Tribes

The USOC is a federation of **Tribes** (the former states, Art. V). Power
divides like this:

**Federal — uniform, non-negotiable (the Twelve Titles, `CATALOG.md`):**
- **Title I — The Covenant:** supremacy of Scripture; the Presidency,
  Council of Overseers, CSC (Art. I–III).
- **Title II — Worship:** the sabbath, the calendar, licensed assembly,
  blasphemy (SSC), idolatry (OIP).
- **Title IV — Persons & Violence:** the capital code, homicide, the
  two-witness rule. Federal because blood defiles *the land* (Num 35:33).
- **Title VI/X — Property & Revenue:** the Tithe and Firstfruits (TFRS);
  restitution schedules (RA).
- **Title VIII — Calendar:** the National Sabbath, the feasts, Atonement.
- **Title XII — War:** classified nearly entire (MCD).
- **Citizenship (Art. VI):** the covenant sign, the baptismal record, the
  Declaration of Sole Worship — federal, because citizenship *is* communion.

**Tribe-level — variable, and the variation is where people flee to or
from:**
- Intensity of purity enforcement (BRC), education specifics, licensing
  regimes, the local moral-credit multipliers.
- Some Tribes are lenient border zones; the **west-coast Tribes are the
  "bastion of sin"** — under-enforcing, media-porous, the place the son
  flees. Their signal bleeds back over the Covenant Firewall.

*Drives:* the son escapes *west* because the law is federal but its
*enforcement* is tribal. Geography is the only mercy the system has, and
it's uneven.

## 2. Citizenship — the covenant sign as the gate

Male citizenship begins with **circumcision on the eighth day** (Gen 17:12,
Lev 12:3; `enforced`, BRC), recorded at birth registration. The mark *is*
the papers. An uncircumcised male is a legal non-person: no ration card,
no school enrollment, no inheritance, no Manna account. Females are
enrolled by **dedication and baptismal record**; their civic existence is
routed through a male head of household (see §8).

Citizenship is confirmed annually by the **Declaration of Sole Worship**
(Art. VI, OIP) — and can be *suspended* by a fallen moral-credit score
(§3). Citizenship is therefore not a status you have; it's a status you
**maintain**, daily, or lose.

*Drives:* the youngest son is a citizen from the eighth day; the state has
a claim on him from before memory. When the father is taken, the child
isn't orphaned — he *reverts to his true guardian*, the state that marked
him.

## 3. Degrees of Sanctity — the moral-credit engine (the class system)

The single most important instrument of daily life. Every citizen carries
a **Sanctity Score** on the Manna (§5): a running moral-credit rating built
from tithe compliance, attendance, confession logs, vaccine *refusal*
(§7), scripture-memory badges, neighbor reports, and consumption. The score
sets **access to resources, employment, medical care, housing, and media
tier** — the "Degrees of Sanctity."

Merit badges are the transcript of the soul. **No badges = worthless to
society.** A high score is a green light; a low score is a slow suffocation
— throttled rations, the Law-Channel-only media tier, the back of every
queue.

**The hidden mechanism (Act-2 payload):** the score is *purchasable*. The
wealthy buy it — through conspicuous tithing, "love-offering" endowments,
Sabbath Exemption Licenses, and the frank cash instruments of §9. In
**Act 1 the law looks universal and just**, and the devout family believes
it (the two-witness rule even *looks* fair). **In Act 2 the family
discovers justice is a tier they couldn't afford** — that the camp, the
disappearance, the deportation were all survivable one Degree up. *"We
would have been fine with more money."* Plant it early: in Act 1, stage a
wealthy family's identical infraction waved through — unregistered by the
family and the audience until the reveal detonates it.

*Drives:* the whole tragedy. This family is **middle-sanctity** — devout,
sincere, unrich. They did everything right and it was never going to be
enough, and they don't learn that until it has cost them everyone.

## 4. Who actually rules — the wealth triangle

No secular parties. **The denominations are the parties** (the blocs of
`DenominationalNotes.md`), and their power is three different *kinds* of
wealth, which is why none can simply win:

- **The Evangelicals — cash, media, votes, moral-credit.** Prosperity-
  gospel megachurches; liquid money; they own the Manna (§5); they dominate
  the elected/populist sphere because money-is-speech rewards liquid
  wealth. Nouveau, flashy, insecure, ascendant. *Your prosperity-grift
  pastors.*
- **The Catholics/Orthodox (Liturgical bloc) — assets, frozen.** The
  richest in raw holdings — centuries of land, art, hospitals,
  universities — but their wealth is **illiquid and suspect**: Leviticus
  25:34 makes church-lands *inalienable* (already `deferred`/`adapted` in
  the catalog), so they are land-banks that cannot sell, and the OIP keeps
  them in permanent idolatry litigation over their imagery. **Rich and
  caged.** *Your surveilled-but-essential hospital staff; the underground's
  quiet financiers.*
- **The Reformed — doctrinal control.** The least flashy money, the most
  structural power: they run the **CSC**, the machine that certifies what
  Scripture means and therefore **what money is allowed to buy.**
  Kingmakers. *Your cold clerics who sentence the father.*

Cash (Evangelical) vs. assets (Catholic) vs. doctrine (Reformed). The
Evangelicals win the visible country; the Catholics are the richest and
least free; the Reformed hold the veto. Every faction scene lives in this
triangle.

*Drives:* the pastor who profits, the hospital that hides people, the
tribunal that condemns — three denominations, three kinds of power, one
family caught between them.

## 5. Media — "the Manna" (techno-feudalism)

Because the Evangelicals hold the cash and the platforms, **public media is
theirs**, and it is one integrated system — the play's techno-feudalism
made literal:

- **The Manna** — a single church-state-corporate platform fusing
  broadcast + the Sanctity Score + ration cards + licensing. Your screen
  shows your tier, your rations, the day's statutes, and prosperity
  programming. **Access is gated by your score:** high tiers get rich
  content; low tiers are throttled to the **Law Channel** — 24/7 recitation
  of the statutes (*"Daily Obedience, Daily Manna"*).
- **Licensing (all media "Christ-Aligned").** The Sacred Speech Commission
  licenses every outlet, creator, and household "witnessing" account;
  licenses are credit-gated. The two capital media sins: **"worldliness"**
  (1 John 2:15, `enforced`) and **"false prophecy"** (unlicensed religious
  claims). Lose the license → digital unpersoning.
- **The Covenant Firewall.** Outside media is contraband (Iran/China
  firewall + the Brexit hard border). It leaks: smuggled drives, the
  **west-coast signal bleeding over the border**, samizdat. Licensed
  exceptions: redacted foreign intel for officials; and **weaponized**
  outside media — "know your enemy" clips shown in re-education.
- **The panopticon.** The Manna watches back. Churches, schools, and
  grocery stores are its nodes; your consumption updates your score. The
  surveillance is not bolted on — it is the *same system that feeds you.*

*Drives:* the Manna is how the son's Sunday-school comment is logged, how
the father's smuggled letters and unleavened cookies are caught, and how
the son — by Year 10 — becomes a voice *on* it. The thing that raised him
is the thing that took his father.

## 6. Education — school is church

Deuteronomy 6:7 ("teach them diligently unto thy children") is `enforced`;
the **National Devotion Service** runs the schools. Every day is Sunday
school; **merit badges are the transcript** (and feed the Sanctity Score);
science is curtailed (Genesis over evolution — "conflicts with science" is
literally an SAB category in our index). Homeschooling is legal but
curriculum-audited for "scripture density." Re-education camps are the
system's continuation for older children and adults — not an aberration but
*graduate school.*

*Drives:* the youngest son's "wrongthink" is caught at school because
**catching wrongthink is what the school is for**; his re-education is the
same institution, escalated. He graduates into a true believer.

## 7. Bodies, medicine, opt-outs

- **Opt-outs are purchased privileges, not rights.** Nothing sacramental
  can be declined; everything else can be *exempted* — for a price in cash
  or sanctity. Exemption is a class marker (§3).
- **Vaccines — inverted.** Refusal is a **piety signal that raises your
  score** ("the body is a temple," divine healing over pharma); vaccines
  derived from fetal cell lines are **banned outright** by the same fetal-
  personhood doctrine that drives the abortion law (§ below). The faithful
  poor refuse and sicken; the elite quietly vaccinate on trips to the
  "bastion of sin." *(Brexit bleed: the architects dodge what the believers
  swallow.)*
- **Reproductive doctrine — the Fetal Life Act.** Built on Exodus 21:22-25
  read for total fetal personhood (via the Septuagint "harm follows →
  life for life"), against the plain Hebrew that prices the fetus as a
  *fine*. All abortion is murder; **all IVF embryos are to be destroyed**
  as unlawful life "unless divine circumstances intervene from the
  Authority"; **IUI is permitted only with the husband's own seed** (donor
  seed = adultery/lineage violation). The Numbers 5 *sotah* ordeal — the
  state ritually *ending* a pregnancy it deems illegitimate — sits in the
  same code, unreconciled. See the scene-brief (forthcoming
  `PlayBible.md`).

*Drives:* the daughter's arc lives entirely here — forced to carry (Ex
21:22 weaponized), then put on trial by the same code (*sotah*) when
violence ends the pregnancy. The contradiction is written on her body.

## 8. Women — franchise on paper, life under headship

- **Vote:** the 19th Amendment is "received" (Const. Art. X §2) — but
  hollow. Women retain the franchise (the regime needs the "we're not
  backward" cover, and needs them at the Septennial Reading, Deut 31:12),
  while **household-headship voting** and social pressure make it nominal.
  *You may vote; the outcome is managed.*
- **Office:** 1 Timothy 2:12 — `enforced`. **No woman holds clergy office
  or a Council seat.** The entire ruling apparatus is male by law. Women's
  ordination is the underground's largest denomination.
- **Work:** permitted but at a husband's sufferance. **Proverbs 31** is the
  proof-text that women *may* labor — in a bounded sphere: textiles, food,
  midwifery, nursing, teaching young children, the purity administration
  (largely women governing women, FPA). But **Numbers 30** lets a husband
  void his wife's vows and contracts, so her economic life is revocable.
- **The destabilizing exception:** the widow and divorcée (Num 30:9) have
  *full* legal capacity. Unmarried women are freer than married ones —
  which the state finds threatening and pressures into remarriage.

*Drives:* the trad-wife mother has no independent legal voice — her
attempt to intervene for her husband is, by the code, a wife acting
outside headship, which is why it reads as **sedition** and gets her
deported. The daughter, sent home pregnant and unmarried, briefly holds
the widow's-paradox autonomy — and the state's answer to an autonomous
woman it cannot place is to grind her until she chooses the pier.

## 9. Race — confessional in doctrine, segregated in practice

Officially the covenant is **confessional, not racial**: a Black or Latino
evangelical is fully a citizen if communicant, and the regime *uses* this
to deny it is racist. Beneath the doctrine: the endogamy statutes (Deut
7:3, anti-intermarriage) are racially weaponizable; congregations
self-segregate; "uninclusive communities" are tolerated as local custom.
Diversity with a rotting substrate — the covenant transcends race in the
catechism and enforces it at the county line.

**The Liberia problem (flag for intentionality):** deporting the wife to
*Liberia* — the historical Black-repatriation colony — is a loaded choice.
Read one way it is the regime weaponizing the machinery of *racial* exile
against a white dissident (a deliberate, grotesque irony); read another it
reveals where the regime sends everyone it deems "not of the nation."
Either is powerful; the play should choose knowingly, because an audience
will read it hard. *(Better than* The Handmaid's Tale*, which was faulted
for erasing race — this play can make it explicit.)*

*Drives:* the mother's destination is not neutral. Where she is sent says
what the regime thinks she *is.*

## 10. Due process — the spectrum, and where the USOC lands

Real confessional states run a spectrum of criminal procedure, most
protective to most brutal:

| End | Real model | Character of due process |
|---|---|---|
| More protective | Israel (religious-court enclaves in a democracy) | civil courts, appeals, counsel; religion governs personal status only |
| Dual | Malaysia, Pakistan | parallel civil/religious courts; blasphemy law but formal procedure, appeals |
| Clerical-authoritarian | **Iran** (Revolutionary Courts) | clerical judges, coerced confession, limited counsel, public execution — *procedure as theater* |
| Discretionary | Saudi Arabia (*ta'zir*) | judge's discretion over uncodified offenses; religious police |
| Summary | Taliban, ISIS quasi-state | no counsel, no appeal, corporal/capital on the spot |
| Historical Christian | Geneva Consistory; Puritan Salem; the Inquisition | the Inquisition's *procedure* (secret witnesses, confession, the auto-da-fé) is the nearest ancestor |

**Where the USOC lands: variable by class, and hidden until Act 2.** On
paper the procedure is *protective* — the two-witness rule (Deut 19:15),
no ransom for murder (Num 35:31), the forty-stripe dignity cap (Deut 25:3)
all read as restraint. In practice every safeguard is subverted (the
professional-witness market, selective enforcement), and the *tier* you
receive depends on your Sanctity Score and your cash:

- **The wealthy get Iran** — procedure, delay, counsel, a ransom (Ex 21:30
  Life Ransom), a charge that quietly evaporates.
- **The poor get the Taliban** — summary re-education, disappearance, the
  camp, the pier.

Act 1 shows the protective face and lets the family (and audience) trust
it. Act 2 reveals the two-tier truth. The two-witness rule is the perfect
emblem: a *genuine* evidentiary safeguard, defeated by a market in
witnesses only the connected can afford — or weaponized by neighbors and
children who testify for credit.

*Drives:* the father's end. He is taken not by a mob but by *process* — a
report (his own son), a witness, a charge (unleavened cookies, smuggled
letters), a tribunal. The horror is that it is *lawful*, and that at a
higher Degree it would have been a fine.

## 11. Immigration — Title XI, the real argument onstage

Title XI (Aliens & Strangers) is deliberately the thinnest Title, and it
stages the **actual** American-Christian immigration debate. The regime
deports the stranger *with Scripture* — Romans 13 (obey the authorities),
"a nation has borders," "render unto Caesar" — while dissidents resist
*with Scripture* — "thou wast a stranger in Egypt" (Ex 22:21), "the
stranger... thou shalt love him as thyself" (Lev 19:34), "I was a stranger
and ye took me in" (Matt 25:35). The plan is to wire **real quotes from the
present-day debate** (e.g., the article *"The Bible is not a policy
manual"*) into the law and the characters, so the politics play as
documentary, not invention. The "one law for the citizen and the stranger"
clause (Num 15:16) exists — quietly moved to an appendix.

*Drives:* the son's beloved is an immigrant; her family's status is
adjudicated under Title XI. The nation that rehearses the Exodus nightly
(Deut 16:3) cannot abide an actual stranger — and the boy who loves one
must choose the "bastion of sin" over the covenant.
