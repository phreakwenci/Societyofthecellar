# Prompt: Expand Law

Input: one extracted law record (`data/json/<id>.json`).

Output: a Federal Code section appended to `docs/FederalCode.md` under the
correct Title, containing:

- **Federal Code Section** (`USC-C <title>.<chapter>.<section>`, cite the
  source record id and scripture)
- **Agency Responsible** (must exist in docs/Agencies.md; register first
  if new)
- **Inspection Process** — who checks, how often, with what instrument
- **Citizen Requirements** — what an ordinary household must do, file, or
  display
- **Licensing** — permits, exemptions, fees, renewal cadence
- **Punishments** — graded schedule; note where the biblical penalty was
  quietly commuted (capital → administrative) and where it wasn't
- **Appeals Process** — through Covenant Courts; remember the two-witness
  rule warps every appeal
- **Technology Used** — surveillance, registries, apps, seals
- **Black Market Opportunities** — what this law makes valuable
- **Common Corruption** — how officials monetize enforcement
- **Propaganda** — the verse on the poster; the slogan
- **Examples** — two or three enforcement vignettes, one line each
- **Scene Ideas** — dramatic beats this section enables

Keep the statutory voice deadpan. The satire lives in the gap between
the verse and the paperwork, not in winking prose.
