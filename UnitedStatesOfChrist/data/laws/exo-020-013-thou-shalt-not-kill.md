# Thou Shalt Not Kill

> *Thou shalt not kill.*  
> — Exodus 20:13 (KJV)

| **Summary** | Prohibits unlawful killing. The Hebrew verb (ratsach) targets murder and manslaughter of persons within the community, not all killing — war, execution, and animal slaughter proceed under other statutes. |
| **Original Audience** | Israelite covenant community; the ban protects members from each other, not enemies from Israel. |
| **Immediate Context** | Sixth commandment; the Covenant Code immediately elaborates it into degrees — murder, manslaughter, refuge (Ex 21:12-14). |
| **Historical Context** | Homicide law is universal in ANE codes; Israel's distinctives are refusal of monetary ransom for murder (Num 35:31, contrast Hammurabi's composition payments) and the blood-pollution theology: unavenged blood defiles the land itself (Num 35:33). |
| **Penalty** | Death for murder (Ex 21:12); exile to refuge for manslaughter (Num 35). |
| **Exceptions** | Capital punishment commanded (Ex 21:12ff), war commanded (Deut 20), and divinely ordered killings throughout the conquest narratives. |
| **Cross References** | • Genesis 9:6<br>• Exodus 21:12-14<br>• Numbers 35:16-34<br>• Deuteronomy 19:1-13 |
| **Related NT Passages** | • Matthew 5:21-22 — Jesus extends the ban to anger and contempt<br>• Matthew 26:52 — 'they that take the sword shall perish with the sword'<br>• Romans 13:4 — the ruler 'beareth not the sword in vain', the state's favorite verse<br>• 1 John 3:15 — hatred as murder |
| **Internal Contradictions** | • To kill or not to kill: the commandment vs. God repeatedly commanding killings (1 Sam 15:3, Deut 7:2) — the SAB's central exhibit. [1 Samuel 15:3, Deuteronomy 7:2, Exodus 32:27] (con109) |
| **SAB Categories** | — |
| **USofC Implementation** | Title IV's cornerstone. The USofC preserves the ratsach reading loudly: murder is capital, but state executions, sanctioned war, and 'covenant defense' are definitionally not killing. Per Numbers 35:31, plea-bargaining a murder charge to a fine is scripturally barred — the one place the regime's courts are genuinely, terrifyingly incorruptible. |
| **Agency** | Covenant Courts |
| **Constitutional Analogue** | Ordinary homicide law, minus Eighth Amendment proportionality and with mandatory capital sentencing. |
| **Scene Ideas** | • A defense attorney's entire practice: relitigating the Hebrew verb, one preposition at a time.<br>• An execution chamber with Matthew 5:21 stenciled over the door by a chaplain who has stopped sleeping.<br>• A prosecutor who cannot offer the plea deal both families are begging for, because Numbers 35:31 forbids ransom. |
| **Satirical Uses** | • The commandment appears on the Department of War's letterhead, untranslated, in Hebrew — so no one can be sure it applies. |
| **Bureaucratic Uses** | • Homicide classification worksheet: murder / manslaughter / covenant defense / sanctioned act<br>• Blood-pollution land audits after unsolved killings (Deut 21:1-9 heifer procedure, still on the books) |
