# No Coveting

> — Exodus 20:17 (KJV)

| **Historical Context** | Unique among ANE laws in criminalizing desire itself; no court could try it, which is precisely the point — it hands enforcement to conscience (or, in a theocracy, to whoever claims to audit conscience). The wife's placement between house and servants is Exhibit A for the property-status of women; Deuteronomy 5:21 pointedly re-orders to put her first and separate. |
