# Remember the Sabbath

> *Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates.*  
> — Exodus 20:8-11 (KJV)

| **Historical Context** | A seven-day rest cycle detached from lunar phases is without clear ancient Near Eastern parallel and became Israel's signature institution. Numbers 15:32-36 shows enforcement: a man gathering sticks on the Sabbath is stoned. The commandment is notably humane toward labor and notably lethal toward violators. |
| **Penalty** | Death (Ex 31:14-15, Numbers 15:32-36); here, none stated. |
| **Exceptions** | Priestly temple labor proceeds (Matt 12:5); later rabbinic principle that saving life overrides the Sabbath; Jesus adds healing and need (Mark 2:23-28). |
