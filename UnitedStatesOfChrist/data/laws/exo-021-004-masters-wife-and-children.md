# Master Keeps the Wife He Gave, and Her Children

> — Exodus 21:4 (KJV)

| **Original Audience** | Israelite slaveholders; the wife in view is the master's servant, whose status transfers to her children. |
| **Immediate Context** | Qualifies the six-year release (v.2) and manufactures the dilemma that v.5-6's perpetual-service election 'solves.' |
| **Historical Context** | Standard ANE property logic — offspring follow the mother's status — but here it functions as a retention mechanism: the family is the leverage that converts a six-year term into voluntary perpetuity. The law's cold machinery is visible in a way later apologetics never quite survives. |
