# No False Witness

> *Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.*  
> — Exodus 20:16 (KJV)

| **Summary** | Prohibits perjured testimony against a member of the community; the integrity clause of a legal system that runs entirely on witnesses. |
| **Original Audience** | Israelites, whose courts had no forensics — two or three witnesses were the whole evidentiary apparatus (Deut 19:15). |
| **Immediate Context** | Ninth commandment; Deut 19:16-21 adds the enforcement: a proven false witness suffers whatever penalty he sought for the accused. |
| **Historical Context** | Hammurabi opens his code (CH 1-4) the same way: false capital accusation is itself capital. When testimony is the only evidence, perjury is the system's single point of failure — both codes know it. |
| **Penalty** | Talionic: 'then shall ye do unto him, as he had thought to have done unto his brother' (Deut 19:19) — up to death for false capital testimony. |
| **Exceptions** | None. (Narratively, God sends a lying spirit in 1 Kings 22:22 — the awkward footnote.) |
| **Cross References** | • Deuteronomy 19:15-21<br>• Deuteronomy 17:6<br>• Numbers 35:30<br>• Proverbs 19:5<br>• 1 Kings 21 (Naboth, the false-witness show trial) |
| **Related NT Passages** | • Matthew 26:59-61 — false witnesses sought against Jesus<br>• Acts 6:11-13 — false witnesses against Stephen<br>• Matthew 18:16 — Jesus imports the two-witness rule into church discipline |
| **Internal Contradictions** | • Does God countenance deception? The absolute ban vs. God sending a lying spirit (1 Kings 22:22) and rewarding the Hebrew midwives' lie (Ex 1:19-20). [1 Kings 22:22, Exodus 1:19-20] |
| **SAB Categories** | — |
| **USofC Implementation** | The load-bearing wall of USofC justice. Because Deut 19:15 requires two witnesses for any conviction, a professional Certified Witness industry emerged — licensed, bonded, and traded. Proven perjury triggers the mirror penalty in full, including capital. The Naboth scenario (a rigged two-witness frame) is the regime's signature abuse and its official nightmare. |
| **Agency** | Covenant Courts |
| **Constitutional Analogue** | Perjury statutes plus the Confrontation Clause — but with a two-witness sufficiency rule the US Constitution reserves for treason alone (Art. III §3). |
| **Scene Ideas** | • A Certified Witness who has 'independently observed' forty crimes this fiscal year, testifying with the serenity of a notary.<br>• The mirror-penalty execution of a false witness, attended by the man he almost hanged.<br>• Two strangers who actually did see the crime, discovering the defense will impeach them for lacking witness licenses. |
| **Satirical Uses** | • The witness exchange has a ticker: corroboration futures rise every election season. |
